# Clause Checklist by Contract Type

Start with the **Universal** list for every agreement, then add the section for the contract type. Not every item applies to every deal — use judgment and skip what is irrelevant, but consider each.

## Contents
- Universal
- NDA / Confidentiality
- Master Services / Professional Services
- SaaS, Software License, Technology
- Employment, Independent Contractor, Separation
- Purchase of a Business (Asset or Equity Purchase)
- Commercial Lease
- Operating / Shareholder / Partnership Agreement
- Settlement & Release
- Amendments

---

## Universal
- Parties: correct legal names, entity types, states of formation, signatory authority
- Recitals: accurate, don't create unintended obligations
- Definitions: complete, consistent, no circularity; capitalized terms all defined
- Term, renewal (auto-renewal + notice window), termination for convenience / cause, cure periods
- Payment: amounts, timing, late fees, taxes, set-off, price escalation, disputed invoices
- Representations & warranties: scope, knowledge qualifiers, survival period, disclaimer
- Indemnification: who, for what, procedure (notice, control of defense, settlement consent), caps, exclusive remedy
- Limitation of liability: cap amount and basis, exclusion of consequential damages, carve-outs (indemnity, confidentiality, IP, gross negligence/willful misconduct, data breach), mutuality
- Insurance requirements
- Confidentiality (if not a standalone NDA)
- IP ownership and licenses
- Assignment and change of control
- Governing law, venue, jury waiver, arbitration (seat, rules, costs, class waiver) — under NJ law the arbitration clause must clearly say the party gives up the right to sue in court (*Atalese*)
- Notices (method, addresses, email validity)
- Force majeure (scope, notice, termination right if prolonged)
- Entire agreement, amendment in writing, order of precedence among documents, severability, waiver, counterparts/e-signature
- Survival clause covers what should survive
- Exhibits/schedules attached and consistent

## NDA / Confidentiality
- Mutual vs. one-way; matches actual information flow
- Definition of Confidential Information — marking requirement? oral disclosures?
- Standard exclusions (public, already known, independently developed, received from third party)
- Permitted use limited to the stated purpose; permitted disclosees (representatives, advisors)
- Compelled-disclosure procedure
- Term of obligations vs. term of agreement; trade secrets protected indefinitely
- Return/destruction, with backup/archival carve-out
- Residuals clause (usually resist as discloser)
- Non-solicitation / non-compete hidden in an NDA
- Remedies: injunctive relief without bond
- Defend Trade Secrets Act whistleblower notice (US, where individuals are bound)

## Master Services / Professional Services
- Scope via SOWs; change-order process; order of precedence MSA vs. SOW
- Acceptance criteria and deemed acceptance
- Service levels and remedies (credits as sole remedy?)
- Personnel: key personnel, subcontracting, background checks, non-solicit
- Deliverable ownership vs. pre-existing/background IP; license back
- Warranty of workmanlike performance; re-performance remedy
- Data protection, security, breach notification
- Independent contractor status

## SaaS, Software License, Technology
- License scope: users, entities/affiliates, territory, purpose; restrictions
- Uptime SLA, support tiers, maintenance windows, credits
- Customer data: ownership, use by vendor (aggregate/AI training), export on termination, deletion timeline
- Security standards (SOC 2, ISO 27001), audits, breach notice timeline
- Privacy: DPA, subprocessors, cross-border transfers, HIPAA BAA if PHI
- Price increases at renewal; usage overages
- IP infringement indemnity with procure/modify/refund remedies
- Escrow or transition assistance for critical systems
- Open source and third-party components; AI output ownership and warranties

## Employment, Independent Contractor, Separation
- Classification risk (contractor vs. employee)
- Compensation, bonus/commission plan terms, equity references
- At-will language vs. term; severance triggers ("cause", "good reason" definitions)
- Restrictive covenants: non-compete, non-solicit — scope, duration, geography; NJ common-law reasonableness test and pending NJ legislation (see nj-contract-law.md)
- NJ: NDA/confidentiality terms vs. N.J.S.A. 10:5-12.8 (discrimination/harassment/retaliation details); ABC test for contractors; no prospective LAD waivers
- Invention assignment with statutory carve-out notices where required
- Release: consideration, carve-outs for non-waivable claims, ADEA/OWBPA timing for workers 40+ (review/revocation periods)
- Non-disparagement; confidentiality carve-outs required by law (e.g., whistleblowing, harassment disclosures)

## Purchase of a Business (Asset or Equity Purchase)
- Structure (asset vs. equity) and what it means for liabilities assumed
- Purchased/excluded assets; assumed/excluded liabilities
- Purchase price, adjustments (working capital), earn-outs (metrics, control, dispute mechanism), escrow/holdback
- Reps & warranties: financial statements, taxes, compliance, contracts, IP, employees, litigation, environmental; disclosure schedules
- Indemnification: basket (deductible vs. tipping), cap, survival, fundamental reps, sandbagging
- Covenants pre-closing; conditions to closing; MAC definition
- Restrictive covenants on seller
- NJ bulk-sale notice: purchaser files Form C-9600 so the Division of Taxation **receives** it ≥10 business days before closing; escrow mechanics; tax clearance letter as a closing deliverable (see nj-contract-law.md)
- Third-party consents, permits/licenses transfer, lease assignments
- Employee matters: offers, accrued PTO, benefit plans
- Closing deliverables checklist

## Commercial Lease
- Premises, rentable vs. usable area, common areas
- Rent, escalations, operating expense pass-throughs (caps, exclusions, audit rights)
- Use clause and exclusives; co-tenancy
- Maintenance/repair split; HVAC; compliance with laws (ADA)
- Assignment/subletting and change of control
- Renewal/expansion options, ROFR/ROFO
- Personal guaranty and its limits
- Casualty, condemnation, rent abatement
- SNDA, estoppel obligations
- Surrender condition, holdover rate

## Operating / Shareholder / Partnership Agreement
- Capital contributions, additional capital calls, dilution
- Allocations and distributions (including tax distributions)
- Management and voting; major decisions requiring consent
- Transfer restrictions, ROFR, tag/drag rights, buy-sell triggers (death, disability, divorce, departure) and valuation method
- Deadlock resolution
- Fiduciary duty modifications; indemnification of managers
- Dissolution and winding up

## Settlement & Release
- Payment terms and consequences of default (consent judgment?)
- Scope of release: parties, affiliates, known/unknown claims (Cal. Civ. Code §1542-type waivers where relevant)
- Carve-outs: obligations under the settlement, non-waivable claims
- No admission of liability; confidentiality; non-disparagement
- Tax treatment and reporting; Medicare considerations for personal injury
- Dismissal mechanics (with prejudice; court retains jurisdiction to enforce)

## Amendments
- Identifies the correct base agreement and all prior amendments
- Changes are precise ("Section 4.2 is deleted and replaced with…")
- "All other terms remain in full force" ratification
- Effective date and whether it is retroactive
