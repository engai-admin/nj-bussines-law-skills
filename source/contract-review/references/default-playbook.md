# Default Negotiation Playbook

Use this only when the firm hasn't supplied its own. It reflects common, middle-of-the-road positions for a **closely held New Jersey business** with ordinary leverage. **Firms should replace this file with their own playbook** — keep the same table shape so the skill can apply it.

For each topic: the preferred position, an acceptable fallback, and the walk-away line that should be flagged High if crossed.

| Topic | Preferred | Acceptable fallback | Flag High if… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limitation of liability (cap) | Mutual cap = fees paid/payable in the prior 12 months | 2× annual fees, or a fixed dollar amount sized to the deal | Client's liability is uncapped while the other side's is capped |
| Cap carve-outs | Indemnity for third-party IP claims, breach of confidentiality, gross negligence/willful misconduct | Super-cap (e.g., 3× fees) for data-breach costs | Carve-outs apply only against the client |
| Consequential damages | Mutual exclusion | Mutual exclusion with same carve-outs as cap | Exclusion protects only the other side |
| Indemnification | Mutual, limited to third-party claims, with notice/defense/settlement-consent procedure | One-way in the other side's favor for matters within client's control only | Client indemnifies for the other side's own negligence or for first-party losses |
| Term & renewal | Fixed term; renewal by mutual written agreement | Auto-renewal with ≥60 days' notice to opt out and capped price increase | Auto-renewal with short (<30-day) notice window and uncapped increases |
| Termination for convenience | Client may terminate on 30 days' notice | 60–90 days, with pro-rata refund of prepaid fees | No termination right for the client but one for the other side |
| Payment terms | Net 45 (client paying) / Net 15 (client paid) | Net 30 | Late fees >1.5%/month or suspension without notice |
| Warranties | Express performance warranty with re-performance or refund remedy | Warranty limited to material conformity with documentation | All warranties disclaimed with no remedy |
| IP ownership | Client owns deliverables made for it; vendor keeps pre-existing IP with broad license to client | Vendor owns, client gets perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license | Client's own data or pre-existing IP assigned or licensed for unrelated use |
| Data use | Use only to provide services; no training of AI models on client data | Aggregated, de-identified data for service improvement | Unrestricted use, sale, or model training on client data |
| Assignment | No assignment without consent, except to successor in merger/sale | Either party may assign to affiliate or successor on notice | Other side may assign freely while client cannot |
| Governing law / venue | New Jersey law; Superior Court in the client's county (or D.N.J.) | Delaware or New York law with NJ venue, or a neutral forum | Distant/foreign forum, or mandatory arbitration with the client bearing costs |
| Arbitration (if used) | Clause states expressly that the parties waive the right to sue in court and to a jury (*Atalese*); AAA/JAMS commercial rules; seat in NJ | Seat in NY/PA | Counterparty-only right to choose court vs. arbitration; no clear waiver language (enforceability risk under NJ law) |
| Bulk sale (client buying assets) | Seller covenants to cooperate with C-9600 notice; Division escrow withheld from price; seller indemnifies for state taxes; closing conditioned on notice | Escrow funded by seller from other funds | No notice mechanics — purchaser exposed to seller's NJ tax liabilities |
| Personal guaranty (client's owners) | None | Limited (capped amount, burn-off after 12–24 months of timely performance) | Unlimited, continuing guaranty of all obligations |
| Non-compete / non-solicit | None, or narrow non-solicit of employees for 12 months | Mutual non-solicit with general-advertisement carve-out; in a business sale, seller non-compete ≤ 5 years tied to the business's actual market | Broad non-compete binding the client or its owners; employee non-competes not tailored to NJ's reasonableness test |
| Confidentiality term | Duration of agreement + 3 years; trade secrets indefinitely | +2 years | No post-termination protection |
