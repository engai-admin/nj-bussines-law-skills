## Choosing the forum
| Forum | Use for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Superior Court, **Special Civil Part** | Money claims up to **$20,000** | Raised from $15,000 eff. July 1, 2022 |
| Special Civil Part — **Small Claims** | Up to **$5,000** | Raised from $3,000 eff. July 1, 2022 |
| Superior Court, **Law Division, Civil Part** | Money damages above $20,000; most contract and business tort cases | Track assignment sets the discovery period (R. 4:5A, R. 4:24-1) |
| **Complex Business Litigation Program** (Law Division) | Complex commercial/construction cases, presumptively ≥ **$200,000** in controversy, designated as complex | R. 4:102–4:103; Admin. Dir. #01-19; motion to include/exclude available |
| Superior Court, **Chancery Division, General Equity** | Injunctions, specific performance, partnership/LLC dissolution, shareholder/member oppression, accountings, restrictive-covenant enforcement | Expedited relief via order to show cause (R. 4:52) |
| **Tax Court** | State tax and local property tax appeals | Separate deadlines |
| **U.S. District Court, D.N.J.** | Diversity (> $75,000, complete diversity) or federal question | Newark, Trenton, Camden vicinages; L.Civ.R. 7.1 motion days |
| **Arbitration** | When the contract requires it | Check the clause's enforceability under *Atalese* first |

Venue: R. 4:3-2 (generally where the cause arose or where any party resides). Confirm county.

## Filing a Superior Court action — checklist
- Complaint with caption (R. 4:5-1, R. 1:4-1)
- **Case Information Statement (CIS)** — required with first pleading (R. 4:5-1(b)(1)); designate track/case type, including CBLP where applicable
- **Certification of no other actions / parties** (R. 4:5-1(b)(2))
- **Certification of compliance with R. 1:38-7** (redaction of confidential personal identifiers)
- Designation of trial counsel (R. 4:25-4)
- Jury demand if desired (R. 1:8-2(b), R. 4:35-1)
- File through **eCourts**; serve summons and complaint (R. 4:4-4)
- Calendar: service, answer date (35 days, R. 4:6-1), track discovery end date → deadline-tracker

## Intake ethics and engagement (NJ)
| Item | Rule | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Conflicts | RPC 1.7, 1.8, 1.9, 1.10 | Run every name on the conflict list; document clearance before substantive work |
| Organization as client | RPC 1.13 | State in the engagement letter that the entity, not its owners/officers, is the client (unless jointly representing, with informed consent) |
| Written fee terms | RPC 1.5(b) | Required in writing when the lawyer hasn't regularly represented the client |
| Contingent fees | RPC 1.5(c); R. 1:21-7 | Must be in writing |
| Retainers / trust funds | RPC 1.15; R. 1:21-6 | Retainers held in trust until earned unless the agreement validly provides otherwise; reconcile monthly |
| Fee disputes | R. 1:20A-6 | Before suing a client for fees: pre-action notice of fee-arbitration rights, then a 30-day wait |
| Threats of criminal charges | RPC 3.4(g) | Don't threaten criminal, administrative, or disciplinary charges to gain an advantage in a civil matter |
| Communications with represented parties | RPC 4.2 | Route through counsel |
| File retention | R. 1:21-6 (trust records 7 years) | Note the firm's retention policy in the engagement letter |

## Entity and transactional touchpoints
| Task | Agency / statute |
|---|---|
| Form an LLC / corporation; amendments; dissolution | NJ Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services (NJ business portal); N.J.S.A. 42:2C (LLC), 14A (corporations) |
| Tax and employer registration (NJ-REG) | Division of Revenue / Division of Taxation |
| Business Registration Certificate (public contracts) | Division of Revenue |
| Annual report (by end of anniversary month) | Division of Revenue; N.J.S.A. 14A:4-5 / 42:2C — confirm |
| Bulk-sale notice (C-9600) on asset sales | Division of Taxation; N.J.S.A. 54:50-38 |
| Tax clearance certificate (some transfers, incentives) | Division of Taxation |
| Liens, judgments, UCC searches for due diligence | NJ UCC filings (Division of Revenue); Superior Court judgment search; county clerk |
| Municipal permits, CO, zoning; liquor licenses | Municipality / ABC |
| Environmental triggers (industrial establishments) | NJDEP — ISRA, N.J.S.A. 13:1K-6 |

## Typical NJ business matter types
Formation and governance; buy/sell of a business (asset vs. equity); operating/shareholder agreements and buyouts; commercial contracts and vendor terms; commercial leases; employment agreements, restrictive covenants, separation agreements; collections and contract disputes; LLC/partner disputes (Chancery); franchise and distribution; HIPAA and other compliance programs for healthcare-adjacent clients; succession planning with the client's advisory team (CPA, financial advisor, insurance, estate counsel).
