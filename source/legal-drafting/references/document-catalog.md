# Document Catalog — Required Elements

Checklist of what each document type must contain. Jurisdiction-specific requirements (court formatting, statutory notices) should be confirmed against local rules.

## Contents
- Client-facing: engagement letter, non-engagement / disengagement letter, client advice letter
- Disputes: demand letter, cease and desist, response to demand, settlement agreement
- Litigation: complaint, answer, motion, brief/memorandum of law, discovery requests, subpoena, litigation hold letter
- Transactional & corporate: NDA, simple services agreement, written consent / resolutions, promissory note, assignment, bill of sale
- Internal: legal research memo

---

## Engagement letter
Client identity (and who is *not* the client — e.g., officers of a corporate client), scope of representation and express exclusions, fee basis and rates, retainer and how it's applied/replenished, expenses, billing frequency and payment terms, staffing, client responsibilities, conflicts disclosure/waiver if any, file retention policy, termination of representation, dispute resolution re fees (some states require notice of fee-arbitration rights — confirm), signature/acknowledgment line. NJ: written fee terms for new clients (RPC 1.5(b)); contingent fees in writing (RPC 1.5(c), R. 1:21-7); entity-as-client statement (RPC 1.13); joint-representation consent where the company and owners are both clients (RPC 1.7); retainer held in trust (RPC 1.15, R. 1:21-6); it is good practice to mention the availability of district fee arbitration (R. 1:20A).

## Non-engagement / disengagement letter
Clear statement the firm does not / no longer represents the person, any limitation periods they should be aware of (without advising on merits), recommendation to seek other counsel promptly, return of documents.

## Client advice letter
Question presented, short answer, relevant facts as understood (invite correction), analysis in plain English, options with pros/cons, recommendation, next steps, privilege legend.

## Demand letter
Sender's representation of client, facts in brief, legal basis (stated conservatively), specific demand (amount/action), deadline to respond, consequences of non-compliance (civil remedies only — NJ RPC 3.4(g)), preservation-of-evidence request if litigation likely, reservation of rights, "for settlement purposes" designation where appropriate (confirm evidentiary rule applies), delivery method (certified mail/email as contract notice clause requires).

## Cease and desist
Identification of the right (trademark, copyright, contract, trade secret), the infringing conduct with specifics, demand to stop and confirm in writing, deadline, reservation of rights. Avoid overreach (bad-faith IP threats can create liability in some contexts).

## Response to demand
Acknowledge receipt, representation, position on facts and liability (deny/without admission), any counteroffer, request for documents, reservation of rights.

## Settlement agreement
NJ: if employee discrimination/retaliation/harassment claims are released, follow N.J.S.A. 10:5-12.8 (no concealing claim details; required notice). Parties, recitals, settlement payment and timing, release (scope, mutual?), no admission, confidentiality, non-disparagement, dismissal mechanics, default remedies, tax, governing law, entire agreement, counterparts.

## Complaint
NJ Superior Court caption (see nj-drafting.md), parties and their residence/state of formation, venue allegations (R. 4:3-2), factual allegations in numbered paragraphs, causes of action (each with elements), prayer for relief, jury demand if wanted, designation of trial counsel (R. 4:25-4), R. 4:5-1(b)(2) certification, R. 1:38-7 certification, Case Information Statement; verification (certification) for Chancery/OTSC matters.

## Answer
Caption, paragraph-by-paragraph admit/deny/lack knowledge, affirmative defenses (consider waiver if omitted), counterclaims/crossclaims, jury demand, certification/signature.

## Motion
Notice of motion (return date, relief, oral argument request if wanted), brief or letter brief, supporting certification (R. 1:4-4(b)) with exhibits, proposed form of order, certification of service; NJ return-date timing R. 1:6-3 (16/8/4 days) or R. 4:46-1 for summary judgment (28/10/4); discovery motions need the good-faith meet-and-confer certification (R. 1:6-2(c) — confirm).

## Brief / memorandum of law
Table of contents/authorities if required, preliminary statement, statement of facts (with record cites), standard, argument with point headings, conclusion with specific relief.

## Discovery requests
Definitions and instructions (reasonable), numbered requests within numerical limits, time period, service certificate. Responses: general objections used sparingly; specific objections stated with particularity; whether documents are being withheld.

## Litigation hold letter
Duty to preserve, categories of documents and ESI (email, texts, messaging apps, cloud), custodians, time period, suspension of auto-deletion, acknowledgment request.

## R. 1:20A-6 pre-action notice (before suing a client for fees)
Certified AND regular mail (or hand delivery); fee committee secretary's name, address, phone for the district; client's right to request fee arbitration; warning that the right is lost if not filed within 30 days of receipt; no suit until 30 days pass.

## Client-education handout
Plain-English title naming the decision; "what this means for you"; short Q&A sections; next-steps checklist; general-information disclaimer with firm name; resources listed as possibilities, not endorsements; 1–2 pages.

## NDA
See contract-review checklist; draft mutual unless asked otherwise; include purpose, definition, exclusions, obligations, compelled disclosure, term, return/destruction, remedies, DTSA notice if individuals bound.

## Asset purchase agreement (NJ add-ons)
Bulk-sale (C-9600) covenant and closing condition; Division escrow and clearance-letter release; seller tax indemnity; permit/license transfer conditions; ISRA applicability; seller non-compete tailored to the business sold.

## Simple services agreement
NJ law/venue; arbitration clause with *Atalese* waiver language if arbitration is used. Parties, services/SOW, fees and payment, term and termination, IP, confidentiality, warranties and disclaimer, limitation of liability, indemnity, independent contractor, governing law, notices, entire agreement.

## Written consent / resolutions (board, managers, members, shareholders)
Entity name and type exactly as registered with the NJ Division of Revenue, body acting, authority for acting without a meeting (operating agreement / N.J.S.A. 42:2C for LLCs; N.J.S.A. 14A:6-7.1 directors, 14A:5-6 shareholders — confirm) and unanimity requirement, recitals (WHEREAS), resolutions (RESOLVED), general authorizing resolution, effective date, signature lines for every required signer, counterpart/e-signature clause.

## Promissory note
Principal, interest rate (check usury limits), payment schedule, maturity, prepayment, late charge, default events and acceleration, security (reference to security agreement), governing law, waiver of presentment, signature.

## Assignment / Bill of sale
Assignor, assignee, assigned rights/assets described precisely, consideration, assumption of obligations (or not), representations (title, no encumbrances), further assurances, governing law.

## Legal research memo (internal)
To/From/Date/Re, question presented, brief answer, facts, discussion (rule, application, counterarguments), conclusion, open research items; mark authorities for verification.
