# Sample Structures

Skeletons for the most common drafts. Tailor every section; these show order and required parts, not final language.

## Engagement letter

```
[DATE]

PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL

[CLIENT NAME]
[ADDRESS]

Re: Engagement of [FIRM NAME] — [Matter description]

Dear [NAME]:

Thank you for selecting [FIRM NAME] (the "Firm") to represent [CLIENT LEGAL NAME] (the "Client"). This letter confirms the terms of our engagement.

1. Client. Our client in this matter is [CLIENT LEGAL NAME], a New Jersey [limited liability company / corporation]. We do not represent its members, managers, officers, or employees individually. [If joint: We will represent both the Company and [OWNER]; see the joint-representation disclosure and consent in Section __ (RPC 1.7).]
2. Scope. We will [specific services]. Our engagement does not include [exclusions — e.g., tax advice, litigation, matters outside [STATE]] unless we agree in writing.
3. Fees. [Hourly rates by timekeeper / flat fee of $[ ] covering [ ] / contingency of [ ]%].
4. Retainer. [Amount], deposited in our attorney trust account and applied to [final invoice / each invoice]; to be replenished [ ]. Any unearned portion will be refunded at the end of the engagement.
5. Costs. [Filing fees, courier, experts, etc.] billed at cost.
6. Billing. Monthly invoices due within [ ] days.
7. Client responsibilities. [Truthful, complete information; timely responses; payment.]
8. Termination. Either party may end the engagement [terms]; files handled per our retention policy ([ ] years).
9. Fee disputes. If a fee dispute arises, you may be entitled to request arbitration through the New Jersey District Fee Arbitration Committee (R. 1:20A).
10. No guarantee. We cannot guarantee any particular outcome.

Please sign and return a copy to confirm.

Sincerely,
[ATTORNEY]

AGREED AND ACCEPTED:
[CLIENT] By: ______  Name: ______  Title: ______  Date: ______
```

## Demand letter

```
[DATE]
VIA [CERTIFIED MAIL, RRR / EMAIL / per notice clause § __]

[RECIPIENT]
[ADDRESS]

Re: [Short description] — Demand for [payment / performance]

Dear [NAME]:

This firm represents [CLIENT] in connection with [matter]. [If recipient is represented, address counsel instead.]

[Facts — 1–3 short paragraphs: the relationship, the obligation, the breach, the amount owed, prior attempts to resolve.]

[Basis — conservative statement: "Under Section [ ] of the [Agreement] dated [ ], you agreed to [ ]."]

[Demand — exact amount/action] no later than [DATE] [business days].

If we do not receive [ ] by that date, our client has authorized us to pursue all available civil remedies, including [filing suit in the Superior Court of New Jersey / arbitration under Section __] seeking [damages, interest, fees where contract/statute permits]. [Do not reference criminal or regulatory complaints — RPC 3.4(g).]

[Preservation — please preserve all documents and electronically stored information related to this matter.]

Nothing in this letter waives any of our client's rights or remedies, all of which are expressly reserved.

Sincerely,
[ATTORNEY]
cc: [CLIENT]
```

## Written consent in lieu of meeting

```
WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE [BOARD OF DIRECTORS / MANAGERS / MEMBERS]
OF [ENTITY NAME], a New Jersey [corporation / limited liability company]

Effective [DATE]

The undersigned, constituting [all / the requisite majority] of the [body] of [ENTITY] (the "Company"), acting pursuant to [LLC: Section __ of the Operating Agreement and the New Jersey Revised Uniform Limited Liability Company Act, N.J.S.A. 42:2C-1 et seq. / Corporation — directors: N.J.S.A. 14A:6-7.1; shareholders: N.J.S.A. 14A:5-6] [VERIFY CITE], hereby consent to and adopt the following resolutions:

[TOPIC]
WHEREAS, [background];
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that [action];

General Authorization
RESOLVED, that the officers [managers] of the Company are authorized to execute all documents and take all actions necessary to carry out the foregoing resolutions, and all prior actions consistent with them are ratified.

This consent may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, and shall be filed with the Company's records.

________________  ________________
[NAME]            [NAME]
```

## Motion (NJ Superior Court)

```
[NJ CAPTION — see nj-drafting.md]

NOTICE OF MOTION [FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT / TO COMPEL / TO DISMISS PURSUANT TO R. 4:6-2(e)]
TO: [Counsel of record]
PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on [return date], at 9:00 a.m. or as soon thereafter as counsel may be heard, the undersigned, attorneys for [movant], shall move before the Superior Court of New Jersey, [Division], [County] County, at [courthouse address], for an Order [relief].
PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that movant shall rely on the accompanying brief and certification of [name]. A proposed form of Order is submitted. [Oral argument is requested / waived unless opposition is filed.]
[Filed/served ≥16 days before return date (R. 1:6-3); SJ ≥28 days (R. 4:46-1) — calculate with deadline-tracker]

MEMORANDUM OF LAW IN SUPPORT
  Preliminary Statement
  Statement of Facts (cite declarations/exhibits)
  Legal Standard [CITE NEEDED / VERIFY CITE]
  Argument
    I. [Point heading stating the conclusion]
    II. …
  Conclusion — [specific relief]

[Signature block]
[Certification in support (R. 1:4-4(b)): "I certify that the foregoing statements made by me are true. I am aware that if any of the foregoing statements made by me are willfully false, I am subject to punishment."] [VERIFY current form]
[Certification of service]
[Proposed form of Order]
```

## R. 1:20A-6 pre-action notice (fee dispute)

```
[DATE]
VIA CERTIFIED MAIL, RRR AND REGULAR MAIL

[CLIENT] [LAST KNOWN ADDRESS]

Re: Outstanding fees — [Matter]

Dear [NAME]:

Our records show an unpaid balance of $[ ] for legal services in [matter] (invoices enclosed).

Before this firm may file suit to collect these fees, New Jersey Court Rule 1:20A-6 requires that we notify you of your right to request fee arbitration. You may request arbitration of this fee dispute by contacting the Secretary of the District [__] Fee Arbitration Committee:
  [Name] | [Address] | [Phone]
Please call the Secretary promptly to obtain the forms. IF YOU DO NOT CONTACT THE SECRETARY AND FILE THE APPROVED REQUEST FORM WITHIN 30 DAYS OF RECEIVING THIS NOTICE, YOU WILL LOSE YOUR RIGHT TO INITIATE FEE ARBITRATION.

[Closing]
[Attorney]
```
[Confirm current rule text and committee contact before sending; calendar the 30-day period.]
