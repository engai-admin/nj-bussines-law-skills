NJ Business Law Skills for Claude (v2, 2026-11-02)

install/  - one ZIP per skill, ready to upload
source/   - the editable skill folders (for customizing or putting on GitHub)

Install on your own Claude account:
  Customize > Skills > + > Create skill > Upload a skill > choose a ZIP from install/
  Repeat for all four. Make sure Settings > Capabilities > "Create and edit files" is on.

Install for a whole firm (Team/Enterprise owner):
  Organization settings > Plugins & skills > Add > Upload a skill (each ZIP)

Before relying on any output: deadlines are marked UNVERIFIED and citations
[VERIFY CITE] until an attorney confirms them.
